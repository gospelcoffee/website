Philosophy

Most coffee shops ask you to choose flavors. Valo asks you to choose coffee.

The bean's natural flavor leads the drink. Nothing is added to the coffee to create the notes you taste.

We start with simple drinks, then let you choose the bean by the flavor it naturally brings to the cup.

We hold every ingredient to the same standard: pure, simple, carefully selected, and always coffee first.

Coffee first

The bean leads the drink. Milk, sweetness, and add-ins support the coffee.

Simple choices

A smaller menu helps us make better drinks. Choose your drink, choose your bean, and we guide the rest.

Made with care

We make your drink right. If it needs an adjustment, we fix it.
